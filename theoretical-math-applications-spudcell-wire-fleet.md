## §1 Method — what counts as "actually applies"

Perg's question in the previous turn, in the assistant's restatement: a theoretical-math event (a new bound, construction, or theorem) is not yet an application. It becomes one only when, in the other subject's own terms, it names an object, a measurement, and a decision the measurement would flip. Until then it is a parallel, not a transfer.

Application form used for every attempt in §4:
- MATH EVENT: the result, with its status (LITERATURE / PREPRINT) and its TH number from `~/workspace/inventory/theories-full-inventory.md`.
- SUBJECT EVENT: an event actually seen on record in SpudCell/mirror life, the wire, or the fleet — with its numbers, not a paraphrase of its vibe.
- PINNED OBJECT: the subject object the math acts on, stated so a measurement can be run on it.
- MEASUREMENT: what is computed or counted, with its null.
- DECISION: what changes if the measurement comes out one way or the other.
- VERDICT: ternary (+1 CONFIRMED / 0 VOID-UNDECIDED / −1 REFUTED) plus ANALOGY / DESIGN where applicable. No attempt below is CONFIRMED except the calibration case C-0, which is included to show the standard.
- SETTLER: the run, computation, or primary-source check that would decide it. None of the settlers in §4 has run. Nothing launches without perg's word per item.

Evidence-class guard (TH-054): pairing does not upgrade evidence class. A PREPRINT math event stays PREPRINT after application; a PROPOSAL subject stays a PROPOSAL; a SECONDARY-REPORTING subject event stays secondary reporting. The Kakeya warning from the record is carried in full: Kakeya/Langlands/Kervaire are explicitly NOT irrational-anchor results by metaphor (TH-140, TH-150), and the one reconstructed Wang–Zahl URL in the theoretical-math report is DISREGARDED.

Sources linked, not replaced: theories inventory (TH numbers), `~/workspace/inventory/spudcell-mirrorlife-verbatim-filed.md` (flags F1–F12), `~/workspace/inventory/spudcell-mirrorlife-pairing-map.md` (§2 pairings P-01–P-16, §7 sweep W-01–W-12).
